Our AI uses your phone's front camera to analyze 14 skin parameters including pore size, sebum levels, texture, dark spots, redness, and hydration. The analysis takes 30 seconds and is completely private — images are processed on-device and never stored or shared.

Yes! GlowSkin Pro is free to download. The free plan includes unlimited AI skin scans, your basic 3-step routine, and weekly skin tips. Premium (₹999/month) unlocks full routines, the ingredient checker, progress tracking, and our complete 50,000+ product database.
How soon will I see results? +
Most users report noticeable changes within 2–3 weeks of consistently following their personalized routine. 94% of users see measurable improvement within 30 days. Results vary based on skin type and starting condition.
Does it work for Indian skin types and climate? +
Absolutely. GlowSkin Pro is used by 2M+ people worldwide and our AI is trained on diverse skin tones and types. The routine builder takes your local climate and humidity into account, and our product database includes brands widely available in India.
